About

A pragmatic, people-centred HR leader

Becky Wilding-Jones is a highly experienced HR Executive who has operated at board and senior leadership level across a range of sectors for more than two decades. She is known for her pragmatic, commercially aware approach to people strategy — building high-performing cultures, enabling organisational change, and ensuring the employee experience is at the heart of every business decision.

Whether leading complex transformations, partnering with CEOs and boards, or building HR functions from the ground up, Becky brings clarity, credibility, and genuine care to every engagement.

  • Trusted advisor to boards and executives
  • Proven track record of sustainable culture change
